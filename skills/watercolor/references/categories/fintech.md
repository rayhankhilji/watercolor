# Playbook: Fintech & payments

Money brands must earn trust before they can be interesting — the best do both.

## What great looks like
- **Trust through precision**: exact numbers, tabular figures, meticulous alignment, tinted (not gray) shadows, cool or deep neutrals.
- **A confident primary** used sparingly for action; often a deep ink (navy/near-black) for authority.
- **Product truth**: real cards, real dashboards, real transaction flows.
- **Regulatory calm**: security, compliance and uptime stated plainly with numbers.
- Consumer fintech (Monzo, Cash App, Revolut) trades some formality for bold color and personality — but numbers still read precise.

## Traps
- Stock "handshake" or "piggy bank" imagery, coin/graph icons, green-arrow-up clichés.
- Crypto-style neon and gradients on a regulated brand.
- Vague claims about saving money with no figures.

## Voice
Clear, exact, reassuring. Short sentences for consumer, longer precise ones for infrastructure. Always numerals. Avoid jargon unless the audience is finance teams.
