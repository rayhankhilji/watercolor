# Playbook: Consumer (B2C) & prosumer

Consumer brands win on feeling and memory; prosumer brands (tools individuals pay for, often used at work) blend that with tool credibility.

## What great looks like
- **One memorable idea** executed everywhere: a color (Cash App green, Headspace orange), a character or illustration style, a sound/motion signature.
- **People and life**, not interfaces: photography or illustration of the moment the product improves.
- **Bigger personality**: more color, more expressive type, more playful motion (springs, overshoot) — as long as it's consistent.
- **Mobile first**: the mobile screenshot is the real homepage; app store presence and app icon matter.

## Prosumer specifics
- Show the individual's workflow and the "aha" in seconds (GIF/video demo).
- Price clarity and a generous free tier are brand signals.
- Warmth + competence: friendly copy, precise product.

## Traps
- Generic happy-people stock photography.
- Startup-pastel gradients with no ownership.
- Trying to look "enterprise" and losing the personality that made people care.

## Voice
Warm, direct, human. Short sentences, second person, contractions, a little wit if the brand earns it. Emotional benefit first, feature second.
